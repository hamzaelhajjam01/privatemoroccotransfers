# Morocco Private Drivers — Car image prompts (for Codex)

Every image shows **one of our own vehicles** doing our job: picking up travellers and driving them across Morocco. The look stays the same as the photos you love: warm golden light and calm, premium editorial style.

## How to use (for Codex)

- Run from the project folder: `C:\Users\boulh\Desktop\Morocco private driver website`
- For each image, **use the reference photo listed** so the car matches the vehicle we sell (same model, same black colour, same body shape). Keep the car's design faithful. Don't change it into another model.
- **Save with the exact filename shown.** Part A replaces images the site already uses, so the website updates by itself.
- One image per request works best.

## Style for every image (add to each prompt)

> Photorealistic editorial travel photography, warm golden-hour light, low-contrast grade with highlights leaning gold and soft deep blacks, calm and premium, like a luxury travel magazine. The car is spotless gloss black. No text, no watermark, no logos added, no readable number plate (plate blank or blurred). No faces visible: people only from behind, in silhouette, or cropped at the shoulders. Moroccan setting, authentic and uncrowded.
